Good to Know

Common Questions

Do you take preorders?

Yes! Use the preorder form above, or call/text/email us directly. We'll confirm your order — no payment needed until pickup.

What if you're sold out when I arrive?

Preordering guarantees you get what you want. Walk-ins are always welcome too, but popular items like cinnamon rolls can sell out early.

Do you sell anywhere besides the market?

Right now you'll only find us at our table at the Packard Farmers Market, Saturdays 9am–2pm, May through October.

Can I request a custom order?

Reach out by phone, text, or email and we'll do our best to accommodate special requests.
